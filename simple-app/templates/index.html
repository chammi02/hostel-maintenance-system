🏢 Our Hostel Facilities & Maintenance System

The University Hostel provides modern residential facilities for undergraduates. To maintain standard living conditions, our automated maintenance system allows students to report faults immediately with photos and exact room numbers.

  • ⚡ Electricians: Ceiling fans, room lights, socket outlets, main breakers.
  • 🪚 Carpenters: Beds, study tables, chairs, door locks, cupboard hinges.
  • 🚿 Plumbers: Taps, bathroom drainage, water tank lines.
  • 🛡️ Sub Warden Oversight: Fast approvals, policy verification, technician assignment.

📋 Hostel Maintenance Regulations & Guest Guidelines

  • Electrical Safety: Only certified campus technicians are permitted to open electrical fixtures. Students must not tamper with wiring.
  • Prohibited Items: High wattage immersion heaters and private air conditioners are strictly forbidden; requests related to unauthorized items will be REJECTED.
  • Presence in Room: When a repair slot is scheduled by a technician, the resident student or a room partner must remain present.
