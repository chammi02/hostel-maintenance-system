📋 Hostel Maintenance Guidelines (Guest Information)

Policy Enforcement
⚠️ Safety Precautions
Only certified campus technicians are permitted to open electrical fixtures. Students must not tamper with main switchboards, breaker boxes, or exposed wiring.
🚫 Prohibited Items
High wattage immersion heaters, hotplates, and unauthorized private air conditioner units are strictly barred; repair requests related to unauthorized items will be immediately REJECTED.
📅 Attendance Policy
When a technician schedules a repair appointment, the resident student or an authorized roommate must be present in the room to grant entry and confirm work completion.
